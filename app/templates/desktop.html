Please open this on your phone

Your membership card is added to the wallet app on your phone, so it needs to be opened on the phone you want to keep it on — not on a computer.

  1. Open the same email on your phone.
  2. Tap Add your card.
  3. Your card is saved to your phone's wallet and stays there.
Nothing has been used up by opening this on your computer. Your card is still waiting for you, and the link works as many times as you need.
